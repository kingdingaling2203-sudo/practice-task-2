In order for us to train on how to avoid things like phishing and social engineering we first have to understand each concept. What is Phishing and how does it work, well Phishing by definition is basically a subset of spam emails that are used to convince individuals that the emails come from a reputable or legitimate source and this is where individuals would then start providing login details for banking, social media and government issued information like ID numbers. This is a very common but effective way where hackers and cyber criminals steal, sell information or even commit identity fraud. These emails are mainly targeted to specific people making them believable, this is also known as spear phishing. The one way to educate individuals on Phishing is having a seminar or a full demo on how phishing works and how to identify it. This is to teach individuals to take the necessary steps when it comes to scenarios like this, so to first call the bank an enquire about the email, they should not just click and open random files, they should have an anti virus/malware software installed on all their devices. When it comes to social engineering, this concept is somewhat linked to phishing and they work hand in hand. Social engineering is using a human emotion to make the phishing email feel and sound more real, attackers use a manipulation technique that exploits the psychology of the individual such as fear, urgency and trust. the one way to prevent this is to only download software from verified sources such as google store
